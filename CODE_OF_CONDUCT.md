# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Loop Craft welcomes people who participate in good faith, regardless of experience level or
background. Be respectful, focus criticism on ideas and observable evidence, explain unfamiliar
terms, and leave room for people to correct mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate
misrepresentation of another person's work, publishing private information without permission,
or repeatedly disrupting a technical discussion after a maintainer has set a boundary.

## Project scope

This standard applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is representing Loop
Craft. Technical disagreement is welcome. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or persistently
disruptive.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an ordinary moderation concern, flag the relevant GitHub content or open a concise Issue. If
the report contains private or sensitive information, use the
[private reporting form](https://github.com/Conradgui/loop-craft/security/advisories/new) and begin
the title with `CODE OF CONDUCT`.

Reports will be reviewed as privately as the selected GitHub channel permits. The maintainer will
consider context, impact, prior behavior and the safety of affected participants before choosing
a proportionate response. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is not acceptable.
